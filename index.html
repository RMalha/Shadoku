Shadoku — The Blindspot Murder Mystery

Shadoku is a murder-mystery logic puzzle. Every case is a crime scene seen from above. Each suspect watches along straight lines until a wall or a tall object blocks the view, and no two suspects may see each other.

Place every suspect from the clues. Exactly one tile is left that nobody can see: that is where the assassin, S. H. Adow, was hiding.

Play the story cases, a new Daily Contract every day, or puzzles made by the community. Free in your browser.
